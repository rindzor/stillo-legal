Frequently Asked Questions

How does Stillo work?

Take a photo or upload one from your gallery. Stillo’s AI analyzes your outfit and gives you a style score out of 100, along with what’s working, what to improve, and who your style twin is.

Is Stillo free?

Your first outfit analysis is free. After that, unlimited analyses and all features are available with a subscription.

What subscriptions are available?

Stillo offers a weekly and a yearly subscription. You can manage or cancel anytime in your iPhone Settings under your Apple ID > Subscriptions.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings on your iPhone > tap your name > Subscriptions > Stillo > Cancel Subscription.

Do I need an account?

No. Stillo works without any sign-up. Your saved looks stay on your device.

How is my data handled?

Your photos are sent securely for AI analysis and are not stored on our servers. Saved looks are kept only on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Your saved looks are stored on your device — you can delete any look inside the app at any time.
